Background

I am a materials scientist and electrochemist with comprehensive experience spanning academic research and industrial semiconductor engineering. I completed my direct Ph.D. (MS + Ph.D.) in Chemical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, with a core focus on the design of semiconducting perovskite halides and their applications in optoelectronic devices.

My academic work involves synthesizing lead-free halide double perovskite compositions, engineering octahedral distortions to achieve broadband white-light emission, and fabricating solar-driven photoelectrochemical water splitting cells. This research has led to a granted Indian patent and first-author publications in peer-reviewed journals such as Commun Mater (Nature), Physical Review Applied, and Inorganic Chemistry.

Transitioning to industrial R&D, I worked as a Quantitative Silicon Researcher in the Ingot and Wafer Technology Group at Reliance New Energy Limited. There, I applied machine learning and simulation models (using Python and FlexSim) directly on live Czochralski crystal growth production floors to build digital twins, classify wafer defects, and detect loss-of-structure (LOS) anomalies in real time.

I am passionate about combining fundamental materials chemistry with computational modeling and machine learning to accelerate sustainable energy technologies. I thrive in collaborative environments where I can mentor junior researchers and design innovative device pathways.
