Scientific Focus

Research & Technological Innovations

Exploring materials synthesis, photoelectrochemistry, silicon crystal growth, and AI-driven metrology.

Granted Patent No. 493546 Commun Mater (Nature)

1. Lead-Free Perovskite Optoelectronics & White Light Emission

Investigated structural distortion mechanisms in lead-free halide double perovskites ($\text{Cs}_2\text{AgIn}_x\text{Bi}_{1-x}\text{Cl}_6$). By combining in-situ electric field excitation with FTIR spectroscopy, we established that octahedral inversion symmetry breaking induces parity transition from forbidden to allowed, driving up to a 144× photoluminescence enhancement and enabling single-material white light emission (13.1% PLQY, ~990 ns carrier lifetimes).

2. Photoelectrochemical (PEC) Solar Water Oxidation Cells

Designed stable photoanodes using vacancy-ordered and double halide perovskites ($\text{Cs}_2\text{AgMCl}_6$, where $\text{M = In, Bi, Sb}$). Constructed a custom photoelectrochemical test cell measuring real-time photocurrent density ($J_{\text{PEC}}$), Mott-Schottky band alignments, and EIS charge-transfer resistance, achieving green hydrogen evolution close to 70% of the theoretical limit.

3. Czochralski Silicon Ingot Growth & Loss of Structure Analytics

Executed quantitative silicon research for high-efficiency solar wafer production. Designed 100+ precision AutoCAD parts for hot-zone thermal insulation, modeled melt convection during seed pulling, and developed an HTML/JS Digital Kaizen Root Cause Analysis (RCA) tool to classify crystal Loss of Structure (LOS) anomalies.

Deep Learning Metrology

4. AI Wafer Defect Classification

Built a PyTorch CNN framework trained on 811,000+ semiconductor wafer maps (WM-811K dataset). Integrated Grad-CAM explainability heatmaps and weighted cross-entropy loss, attaining 94.2% accuracy across Scratch, Donut, Ring, and Center spatial failure signatures.

Clean Energy Microgrid

5. Grid-Free Green Corridor System

Constructed a lab-scale microgrid coupling solar PV panels, multi-chemistry lithium battery storage, an alkaline water electrolyzer for hydrogen generation, and a PEM fuel cell stack for continuous off-grid laboratory power.
