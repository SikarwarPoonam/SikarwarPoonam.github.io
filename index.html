Research Areas

Spanning fundamental materials physics to industrial crystal engineering

Perovskite Optoelectronics

Dimensional tuning, organic spacer engineering, and photophysical property relationships in lead-free halide perovskites.

Solar Water Splitting

High-efficiency photoelectrochemical cells using mixed trivalent halide double perovskite compositions.

Silicon Ingot & Wafer Tech

Czochralski single-crystal growth modeling, thermal hot-zone component design, and defect minimization in live fabs.

AI Metrology Pipelines

Deep learning classification models for wafer defect spatial signature mapping and crystal Loss-of-Structure detection.

Renewable Microgrids

Green corridor system design integrating solar photovoltaics, lithium batteries, and hydrogen PEM fuel cells.
