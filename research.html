Materials Science · Optoelectronics

Perovskite Light Emitting Materials

Optoelectronics

My Ph.D. thesis focused on the design of semiconducting perovskite halides and their applications in optoelectronic devices. I synthesized and characterized a library of lead-free double perovskites (e.g. Cs₂AgInₓBi₁₋ₓCl₆ and Cs₂NaInₓBi₁₋ₓCl₆), establishing a universal relationship between local octahedral distortion and emission lifetimes.

I successfully demonstrated proof-of-concept white-light emitting devices by dip-coating mixed-metal double perovskites with PMMA polymers, yielding broadband emission from 400–750 nm. This research resulted in a granted Indian Patent.

Key Results

  • 144× photoluminescence enhancement and ~1 μs excited-state lifetimes through mixed trivalent cation engineering of [AgCl₆]⁵⁻ octahedral distortion.
  • Established electric-field-driven structural distortions in Cs₂NaInₓBi₁₋ₓCl₆ double perovskites using FTIR spectroscopy.
  • Chemically tuned dimensionality in halide perovskites using alkylammonium spacers for targeted optoelectronic properties.
  • Published in Commun Mater (Nature), Inorganic Chemistry, and ChemPhysChem.

Electrochemistry · Solar Fuels

Photoelectrochemical Water Splitting

Solar energy

I chemically engineered Cs₂AgMCl₆ (M = In, Bi, Sb) halide double perovskite photoanodes for solar-driven photoelectrochemical water oxidation. By optimizing bandgaps and interfacial charge transport, I designed a homemade PEC cell that targets clean hydrogen generation from water.

Through electrochemical diagnostics, including electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) and linear sweep voltammetry (LSV), I resolved surface charge transfer dynamics, enabling photoelectrodes to perform close to theoretical thresholds.

Key Results

  • Achieved solar-driven photoelectrochemical water-oxidation efficiency close to 70% of its theoretical limit.
  • Designed, built, and validated a custom photoelectrochemical cell for solar water splitting.
  • Correlated charge carrier lifetime extensions with improved photo-oxidation performance.
  • Published in Physical Review Applied (2023).

Semiconductor Engineering · Crystal Growth

Silicon Ingot and Wafer Tech

Fab Operations

During my tenure at Reliance New Energy Limited, I managed R&D operations for semiconductor ingot and wafer manufacturing. Working on the production floor of a live Czochralski (Cz) silicon crystal growth fab, I built mathematical models to determine crystal length meeting target resistivity (≤1.5 Ω·cm) as a function of melt dopant concentrations.

I designed over 100 hot-zone thermal components in AutoCAD for crystal pullers, optimizing thermal uniformity, single-crystal formation, and suppressing structural dislocations.

Key Contributions

  • Built dopant-resistivity correlation models for Cz-crystal growth.
  • Designed 100+ thermal hot-zone components in AutoCAD.
  • Evaluated thermal uniformity and melt-solid interface dynamics to control defect formation.
  • Supported shop floor workflow simulations, reducing yield-loss by 10%.

Computer Vision · Machine Learning

AI Metrology & Defect Classification

Machine Learning

To automate semiconductor manufacturing quality control, I engineered an end-to-end deep learning pipeline. Using PyTorch, I trained a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to classify defect spatial signatures (Center, Scratch, Edge-Ring, etc.) across 811,000+ Wafer Bin Maps (WM-811K dataset).

To bridge the gap between black-box neural networks and process engineers, I integrated Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping). This provides heatmaps that visualize the final layer weights, allowing engineers on the floor to diagnose physical root causes of equipment drift.

Key Contributions

  • Engineered PyTorch defect classification pipeline achieving 94.2% accuracy.
  • Resolved severe 5,275:1 dataset class imbalance using weighted Cross-Entropy Loss and under-sampling.
  • Implemented Python real-time Loss-of-Structure (LOS) classification model for Cz crystal growth.
  • Built local HTML/JS Digital Kaizen RCA diagnostics tool for operator yield analysis.

Energy Storage · Sustainability

Renewable Microgrids & Water Purification

Sustainability

I developed and operated a renewable-energy microgrid (Grid-free Green Corridor project) combining solar photovoltaics, lithium-ion batteries, alkaline electrolyzers for hydrogen generation, hydrogen storage, and proton-exchange membrane (PEM) fuel cells.

Complementing my energy research, my early work as a JRF at IIT Roorkee explored water purification, developing methods for the adsorption of arsenic and fluoride from groundwater onto local laterite soils, subsequently stabilizing spent adsorbent in construction clay bricks.

Key Results

  • Operational green corridor microgrid providing resilient, sustainable power.
  • Adsorption parameters established for removing arsenic and fluoride from drinking water sources.
  • Awarded top 10 ideas in "New Generation Ideation Contest" by HPCL Green R&D (desalination).
