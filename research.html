1. Lead-Free Perovskite Optoelectronics & White Light Emission

Investigated structural distortion mechanisms in lead-free halide double perovskites (Cs2AgInxBi1−xCl6). By combining in-situ electric field excitation with FTIR spectroscopy, we established that octahedral inversion symmetry breaking induces parity transition from forbidden to allowed, driving up to a 144× photoluminescence enhancement and enabling single-material white light emission (13.1% PLQY, ~990 ns carrier lifetimes).

2. Photoelectrochemical (PEC) Solar Water Oxidation Cells

Designed stable photoanodes using double halide perovskites (Cs2AgMCl6 where M = In, Bi, Sb). Constructed a custom photoelectrochemical test cell measuring real-time photocurrent density (JPEC), Mott-Schottky band alignments, and EIS charge-transfer resistance, achieving green hydrogen evolution close to 70% of the theoretical limit.

4. AI Wafer Defect Classification

Built a PyTorch CNN framework trained on 811,000+ semiconductor wafer maps (WM-811K dataset). Integrated Grad-CAM explainability heatmaps and weighted cross-entropy loss, attaining 94.2% accuracy across Scratch, Donut, Ring, and Center spatial failure signatures.
